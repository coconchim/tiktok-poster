[TikTok Multi Poster]

[TikTok Multi Poster] is a desktop application that helps creators and social media managers schedule and publish their own videos to TikTok accounts they own or are authorized to manage. It uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Features

  • Connect multiple TikTok accounts through official TikTok authorization
  • Publish videos immediately or schedule them for later
  • Choose the cover frame (thumbnail) of each video
  • Track publishing status for every post

How it works

The app runs entirely on the user's own computer. Account authorization data is stored locally and encrypted, and videos are uploaded directly from the user's computer to TikTok.
