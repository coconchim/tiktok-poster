Privacy Policy

App: [TikTok Multi Poster]  |  Last updated: October 6, 2026

[TikTok Multi Poster] ("the App") is a desktop application that lets users schedule and publish their own videos to TikTok accounts they own or are authorized to manage, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. Information the App handles

  • TikTok account authorization data: when you connect an account, TikTok provides your open ID, display name, and access/refresh tokens, according to the permissions you approve (user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload).
  • Videos and post settings: video files you select, captions, privacy level, thumbnail timestamp, and scheduling information that you enter in the App.

2. Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally on your own computer. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your data, and the App contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to connect your TikTok accounts and to upload and publish the videos you choose, at the time you choose. Videos are sent directly from your computer to TikTok through TikTok's API.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. The only party that receives your data is TikTok, as required to perform the posting actions you request. TikTok's handling of data is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

5. Retention, revoking access, and deletion

  • Remove an account inside the App to delete its stored tokens from your device.
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (wording may vary by TikTok version).
  • Deleting the App's installation folder removes all locally stored data.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region).

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date when we do.
